# Atomic Habits — 90-second motion summary

`atomic-habits.mp4` — vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, 90 s, with a synthesized soundtrack.

- `anim.js` — the whole animation, drawn on a canvas as a pure function of time. Open `index.html` to preview it live.
- `music.py` — generates the music and sound effects (numpy only).
- `render.js` — captures frames with Playwright and pipes them into ffmpeg.
- `build.sh` — runs everything and writes `atomic-habits.mp4`.

Scenes: intro · 1% rule · plateau of latent potential · systems vs goals · identity-based habits ·
habit loop · four laws (and their inversions) · habit stacking · environment design · two-minute rule ·
never miss twice · outro.
