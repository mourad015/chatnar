Mourad Ghalbi personal brand. Logo, colors, type and the official video outro, in one place. Use these exact values everywhere.

01

Logo

A red bolt and the stacked wordmark. The bolt stands for speed and energy; the stacked name keeps it compact. Always use the supplied files, never retype the name.

Primary. On brand navy. Use this by default.
Light backgrounds. Wordmark switches to navy.
Icon. The bolt alone, for avatars and favicons.
02

Clear space and size

  • Clear space: keep at least X free on every side, where X is the height of the capital "M".
  • Minimum size, screen: 140 px wide for the full logo, 24 px for the icon.
  • Minimum size, print: 35 mm wide for the full logo.
  • Video: the logo should span about 70% of the frame width in 16:9 and about 90% in 9:16.
  • Backgrounds: brand navy first, white second. On photos, put the logo on a navy plate.
